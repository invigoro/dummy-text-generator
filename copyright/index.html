Copyright and takedown requests

Jabberwock invents its words, but each language takes its flow from a real text: how long its sentences run, how they’re punctuated, which words keep coming back. Every one of those texts was chosen because it’s in the public domain, and SOURCES.md records where each one came from.

If you believe something here uses a work whose copyright you hold, email donotshowmyemail@gmail.com. Please include:

  • the work, and that you own its copyright or act for whoever does
  • where it turns up here: the language, the source text named under it, or a share link
  • how to reach you

Anything that turns out not to be in the public domain will be taken down.
